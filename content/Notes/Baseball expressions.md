A non-exhaustive list of expressions that made their way into the lingo, including in non-baseball country.

>[!Example] List
>- Ballpark approximation (“it’s in the right ballpark”)
>- “Ball hiding”: not sharing all the relevant info
>- “Throwing a curveball”: intentionally causing difficulties
>- “Out of left field”: unexpected
