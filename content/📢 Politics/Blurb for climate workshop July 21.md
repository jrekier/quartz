- the environmental catastrophe continues to unfold in the background since the height of the “strike for climate movement” in 2018-2019.
- pushed off central stage by Covid, imperialist wars, economic downturns, etc.
- The failure of this movement to win substantial gains has pushed layers of activists to explore alternative approach, both organizationally and theoretically. 
- This research is an opportunity but also poses a challenge to the socialist movement, engaging us to sharpen our approach on environmental matters. 
- This workshop proposes to review the state of the crisis and the evolutions of the movements in the past years.
- Also an overview of the ongoing debate within the Marxist tradition on the environment, in particular surrounding degrowth
- contribute to comradely discussion, first step to arriving at a working consensus, and an efficient guide for action.
