"[Housing first](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_First) is a policy that offers unconditional, permanent housing as quickly as possible to [homeless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homelessness "Homelessness") people, and other supportive services afterward."

Keeping researchers precarious makes them less imaginative and incentives cutting corners in their research. I think research would work much better if they could be stabilised early in their careers. 

I guess the obvious argument against that is that it would be too expensive, although I would like to see an honest estimation of all costs and benefits. Regardless, it all depends on how we set our priorities.
