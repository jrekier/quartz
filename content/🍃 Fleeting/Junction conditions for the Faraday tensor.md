In classical electrodynamics, we have the Maxwell's junction conditions: 
$$
\mathbf{n}\cdot[\mathbf{B}]=0
$$
$$
\mathbf{n}\cdot[\mathbf{D}]=\Sigma
$$
$$
\mathbf{n}\times[\mathbf{H}]=\mathbf{K}
$$
$$
\mathbf{n}\times[\mathbf{E}]=\mathbf{0}
$$
where $\Sigma$ and $\mathbf{K}$ are surface charges and current, respectively. 
Some authors* have argued that the last condition should read instead $\mathbf{n}\times[\mathbf{E}+\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}]=\mathbf{0}$.
