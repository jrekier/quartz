The gravity field of Europa and Ganymede deforms under the effects of tides. This effect is captured by the $k_2$ Love number.

>[!Fact] Europa
>If there is an ocean, $k_2\approx 0.23$, if there isn't, $k_2<0.015$ [@MazaricoEtAl2023]

>[!Fact] Ganymede
>If there is an ocean, $0.3\lessapprox k_2\lessapprox 0.6$ [@Jara-OrueVermeersen2016].
